# Stakeholder analysis: Realtime Quiz platform

## Priority stakeholders (High Influence, High Interest)

The two stakeholders requiring the Project Manager's primary and most continuous attention are the **Head of the IT department (Sponsor, S01)** and the **Supervising lecturer (Project Manager, S02)**, both evaluated as High Influence and High Interest (`H, H`). The Sponsor holds ultimate authority over funding, scope baselines, phase gate approvals, and institutional resource allocation; without the Sponsor's sustained engagement and satisfaction, the project risks immediate cancellation or defunding. The Project Manager drives day-to-day coordination, monitors schedule and budget variances, and mentors the student team; maintaining tight alignment between the Sponsor and PM ensures that emerging risks are addressed early, phase gates are scheduled smoothly, and deliverables align precisely with academic and departmental priorities.

## Veto stakeholder

The **Data protection officer (S08)** represents the critical "veto" stakeholder on the project because under university policy, no software application handling student records and grades can be deployed into classroom use without formal data privacy approval; withholding compliance sign-off in week 13 would legally and procedurally prevent the live pilot in week 14 from occurring regardless of whether the software is technically complete.
